Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026  |  Batanes State College, Basco, Batanes

FacultyPulse is the faculty teaching-effectiveness evaluation system of Batanes State College (BatSC), used for the Student Evaluation of Teachers (SET) and Supervisor Evaluation of Faculty (SEF) under CHED CMO No. 19, s. 2025. This policy explains what personal information the system handles, why, and what your rights are under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

In short: your ratings and comments are stored without your name or Student ID. Faculty see only averages and unattributed comments. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

1. What we collect

WhoInformation
StudentsStudent ID, full name, school-provided or personal email address, program/section, enrolled courses, an encrypted (hashed) password, and sign-in activity such as last login time and failed sign-in attempts.
Faculty, supervisors, QA and managementName, email address, department, academic rank, employment type, role, course assignments, and supervisor assignments.
EvaluationsNumerical ratings and written comments (see section 3), and a record that a student has submitted an evaluation for a course.
Evaluation reassignment requestsIf you report a wrong faculty or course, your Student ID and name are attached to the request so the QA Office can verify and fix it. These requests are not anonymous.

2. Why we use it

  • To verify that only enrolled students and authorized staff can sign in, including sending one-time sign-in codes to your email.
  • To make sure each student evaluates each of their courses once, and to let the QA Office follow up on students who have not yet submitted.
  • To compute evaluation results and generate faculty reports required under CHED CMO No. 19, s. 2025.
  • To keep the system secure, fix errors, and correct enrollment or assignment mistakes.

We use your information only for these purposes. By signing in and using the system you acknowledge this policy.

3. How evaluation anonymity works

  • Your ratings and comments are saved without your name or Student ID attached to them.
  • The system separately records only that you submitted an evaluation for a course (and when), so you cannot submit twice. The QA Office can see this status, but not what you rated or wrote.
  • Faculty and supervisors see averages and comments shown exactly as written, without student identity.
  • Because comments are shown as written, please do not include your name or other personal details in a comment. In a small class, the wording itself could point to you.

4. Who can see your information

  • You: your own dashboard and the courses you are enrolled in.
  • QA Office / system administrators: user records, enrollment, and submission status needed to run the evaluation.
  • Faculty and supervisors: results and comments about the faculty member, without student identity.
  • College management: summary results for released reports.

Access is restricted by role. We do not share personal information with other third parties except the service providers below, or when required by law.

5. Service providers

The system runs on cloud services that process data on the college’s behalf. Some of these providers may store or process data outside the Philippines.

  • Supabase — database and account sign-in.
  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Brevo — delivery of sign-in and password-reset emails (receives your email address and the message).
  • Google Fonts — loads the page fonts, so Google can see your IP address when a page loads.

6. Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Student passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text, and repeated wrong attempts temporarily lock the account.
  • Sign-in uses an emailed one-time code, and access to data is limited by role.

No system is completely secure. Keep your password private, change the default password the first time you sign in, and tell the QA Office right away if you think your account was used by someone else.

7. Cookies and browser storage

We store your sign-in session in your browser so you stay signed in while using the system. We do not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers.

8. How long we keep it

We keep evaluation records and user accounts for as long as needed to complete the evaluation cycle and meet the college’s records requirements. Accounts of students who are no longer enrolled are archived and can no longer sign in.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your personal data is handled, and to access it;
  • ask for corrections to inaccurate information, such as a wrong name, email, section, or course;
  • object to processing, or ask for your data to be blocked or removed, where the law allows (some records must be kept for the evaluation process and the college’s records);
  • be indemnified for damages if your data is misused;
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

10. Contact

For questions, correction requests, or privacy concerns, contact the Quality Assurance Office bscqualityassuranceoffice@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
